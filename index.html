About VLB

A family firm, three decades on site

Founded and led by the same family for over thirty years, VLB has grown from a Saharanpur contracting firm into a top-class registered government contractor trusted with large public works.

We build the roads people drive on, the drains that keep towns dry, and the buildings where government serves the public.

Every project is run to the department's drawings, specifications and timelines, with our own site teams, supervisors and machinery.

  • Specification-first execution. Work done to department drawings, schedules and quality norms.
  • Local roots. Based in Saharanpur, with deep knowledge of working conditions across Western Uttar Pradesh.
  • Own teams on site. Experienced engineers, supervisors and labour managed directly by VLB.
  • Timely handover. Planned mobilisation and daily progress tracking until completion.

How we work

From tender to handover

The same disciplined sequence on every government work we take up.

  1. Tender & award

    Careful study of the scope, drawings and BOQ before every bid.

  2. Mobilisation

    Site setup, material sourcing, machinery and teams deployed quickly after award.

  3. Execution & QC

    Work to specification, with regular quality checks and progress reporting.

  4. Measurement & handover

    Joint measurements, documentation and handover to the department.
