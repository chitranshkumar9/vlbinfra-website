About VLB

We build the roads people drive on, the drains that keep towns dry, and the buildings where government serves the public.

Founded and led by the same family for over thirty years, VLB has grown from a Saharanpur contracting firm into a top-class registered government contractor trusted with large public works.

Every project is run to the department's drawings, specifications and timelines, with our own site teams, supervisors and machinery.
