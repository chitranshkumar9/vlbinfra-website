About VLB

We build the roads and bridges people travel on, the canals that water the fields, and the schools and offices where government serves the public.

VLB is an A+ / Class 1 registered government contractor, trusted by State and Central Government departments across Delhi NCR with roads, bridges, irrigation works and public buildings.

Every project is run to the department's drawings, specifications and timelines, with our own site teams, supervisors and machinery.
