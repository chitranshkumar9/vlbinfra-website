About VLB

A+ / Class 1 government civil contractor executing public works for State and Central Government departments across Delhi NCR.

Our story

Building the public infrastructure of Delhi NCR.

Vaibhav Laxmi Builders is an A+ / Class 1 registered government contractor, trusted by State and Central Government departments across Delhi NCR with roads, highways, bypasses, bridges, irrigation works, schools, colleges and government buildings.

We work to the department's drawings, specifications and timelines, with our own engineers, supervisors, labour and machinery on every site.

How we work

What sets VLB apart

The standards we hold on every government work we take up.

  • Specification-first execution. Work done to department drawings, schedules and quality norms.
  • 100% on-time delivery. Every work handed over to the department on schedule.
  • Own teams on site. Experienced engineers, supervisors and labour managed directly by VLB.
  • Timely handover. Planned mobilisation and daily progress tracking until completion.
Process

From tender to handover

The same disciplined sequence on every work.

  1. Tender & award

    Careful study of the scope, drawings and BOQ before every bid.

  2. Mobilisation

    Site setup, material sourcing, machinery and teams deployed quickly after award.

  3. Execution & QC

    Work to specification, with regular quality checks and progress reporting.

  4. Measurement & handover

    Joint measurements, documentation and handover to the department.
